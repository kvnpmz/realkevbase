Refund policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

A printable is a file, not a parcel: once it has been downloaded it cannot be returned. So the rule is simple — if the file is wrong, broken or never arrived, you get it fixed or you get your money back. If it is simply not to your taste, that is harder, but ask anyway.

When we refund in full

  • The PDF will not open, or opens as a blank or damaged file
  • The file is not the guide you ordered
  • You were charged twice for the same order
  • You paid and the download link never arrived, and we cannot reissue it
  • The guide is materially different from how it is described on its page

When we replace instead of refund

An expired download link, a file that did not finish downloading, or an edition you meant to buy instead of the one you bought. We send the correct file at no cost, so you keep what you paid for.

Change of mind

Because the file is delivered instantly and cannot be returned, sales are final once the PDF has been downloaded. If something has gone wrong that does not fit the list above, write to us and explain — we would rather refund a $5 order than leave someone feeling cheated.

European Union and United Kingdom buyers

Digital content normally carries a 14-day right of withdrawal, which is lost once download begins with your agreement. By completing checkout and downloading the file, you agree to immediate delivery and acknowledge that the right of withdrawal no longer applies. Your statutory rights for faulty or misdescribed goods are unaffected, and the refund conditions above still hold.

How to ask

Email support@realkevbase.com with the order email address and the guide name. We reply within two business days. Approved refunds go back to the original card through Stripe, usually within five to ten business days depending on your bank.
