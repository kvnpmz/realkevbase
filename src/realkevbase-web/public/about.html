About SaveSimple

Last updated 17 November 2026

SaveSimple is a small, deliberately narrow shop: printable PDF guides that take a job people already have to do — packing a bag, tracking bills, feeding a baby, moving into a first flat — and put it on one page you can print and tick off.

What we sell

Around 160 PDF guides, most of them $5, covering money and budgeting, healthy living, moving out and everyday admin. Each one is a print-ready page or two, not an app and not a course. Where a guide has an A4 edition as well as US Letter, both files are delivered with the order.

We also sell a few small-run physical items. They ship separately from the PDFs and share the same cart.

How buying works

  • Add guides to the cart and pay by card through Stripe. Card details go to Stripe directly and never touch our servers.
  • The confirmation email carries the download link. It stays live for 24 hours; if it expires, email us and we will reissue it.
  • No account is needed and there is no subscription. Buy once, keep the file.

Why the shop looks new

The storefront opened in November 2026. The guides themselves were sold elsewhere first, which is why the catalogue is large and the domain is young. If something looks missing or wrong, tell us — a real person reads the mail.

Contact

Anything at all — an order, a broken file, a guide you wish existed: support@realkevbase.com.

We answer within two business days, usually sooner.
