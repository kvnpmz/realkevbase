SaveSimple by RealKevbase

Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

SaveSimple is the printable-PDF shop run by RealKevbase at realkevbase.com. This page explains what we collect when you browse or buy, why we need it, who processes it on our behalf, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • Order details. When a payment succeeds we receive your email address, the items you bought, the amount and currency, and a Stripe checkout reference. We use the email address to send your download links and receipt, and to answer questions about the order.
  • Payment details. Card numbers and other payment credentials go straight to Stripe and never reach our servers. We see whether a payment succeeded, not the card that paid for it.
  • Download activity. A download link carries the order it belongs to. Our API checks that reference and returns a time-limited file link. We do not keep a history of which files each person opened.
  • Technical logs. Our API and hosting provider record the request, IP address, browser and time, kept briefly for security, abuse prevention and debugging.
  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-6FS9CWY5LB) to see which pages are visited, roughly where visitors come from (derived from IP address), what device and browser they use, and which site referred them. It sets first-party cookies. We do not use it to identify you personally and we do not run advertising on this site.
  • Your cart. Items you add are stored in your browser's local storage so the cart survives a refresh. They are sent to us only when you start checkout.

Cookies

Essential cookies keep a Stripe checkout session together while you pay. Analytics cookies (_ga and _ga_*) let Google Analytics count visits and are kept for up to two years. The site does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The shop still works, but your visit will not be counted and a checkout may need to be restarted.

Who processes your data

  • Stripe — payments and receipts.
  • Microsoft Azure — file storage and transactional email delivery.
  • Cloudflare — hosting and delivery of the website.
  • Google — analytics.

These providers process data on our instructions and under their own privacy terms. We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. Some providers may process data outside your country using the safeguards their agreements provide.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as tax and accounting rules require, and while a payment could still be disputed, then deleted. Analytics data is kept for the retention period configured in Google Analytics and then aggregated or deleted. Technical logs are kept for a short period and then discarded.

Your choices and rights

Email support@realkevbase.com to ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to a particular use. We will reply within a reasonable time and may ask you to confirm the email address an order was placed with.

If you are in the UK or the EU, you also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. We may need to keep some records where the law requires it, even after a deletion request.

Children

The shop sells household printables and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be described on this page before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy or an order: support@realkevbase.com.

This policy covers realkevbase.com and the SaveSimple guides. Pinterest, Stripe and Google operate their own services under their own privacy policies.
