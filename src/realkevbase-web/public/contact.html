Contact

Last updated 17 November 2026

One inbox, read by a person: support@realkevbase.com. We answer within two business days, usually the same day.

Before you write

  • Download link expired. Links stay live for 24 hours. Send the email address you ordered with and we will reissue it.
  • File will not open. Our PDFs are standard PDF files with no password. If yours will not open, say which guide and which device — we will send a fresh copy.
  • Nothing arrived. Check the spam folder for a message from realkevbase.com, then write to us. If the payment went through, you are entitled to the file.
  • Wrong file. If the PDF you downloaded is not the guide you bought, we will swap it at no cost.

What to include

Your order email address, the name of the guide, and what went wrong. If you have the Stripe receipt, forward it — it lets us find the order in seconds instead of minutes.

Refund conditions are on the refunds page, and the terms of sale on the terms page. What we do with your data is on the privacy page.
